# Project Brief: Career e-Portfolio

## 1. Professional Overview
- **Name/Title:** Assistant Project Engineer
- **Specialization:** Electrical & Electronic Engineering background
- **Objective:** Showcase academic achievements, career progression, technical and project management skills to peers, employers, and clients.

## 2. Goals & Success Criteria
- **User Engagement:** High readability, clean structure, and clear visual hierarchy to retain visitor interest and encourage deep-diving into details.
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Clean, minimalist design. A harmonious, professional color palette (e.g., slate gray, deep steel blue, and muted teal) to represent electrical engineering/precision without clashing or causing eye strain.
- **Performance:** Lightweight, fast load times, and mobile-responsive.

## 3. Required Sections
1. **Header / Hero Section:** Introduction as Assistant Project Engineer.
2. **About Me:** Brief professional statement aligning engineering background with project management.
3. **Academic Background:** Degree, certifications, and educational highlights.
4. **Work Experience Timeline:** Step-by-step career path, detailing responsibilities and projects.
5. **Skills Inventory:** Categorized list of technical (Electrical & Electronic) and management/software tools.
6. **Contact Section / Footer:** Simple ways to get in touch, links to LinkedIn/GitHub/Email.

## 4. Key Constraints & Out-of-Scope (Non-Requirements)
- **No Complex Database:** The site will be static (HTML/CSS/JS) to ensure ease of hosting on Netlify.
- **No Heavy Animations:** Subtle micro-interactions (e.g., hover transitions) are allowed, but heavy/distracting animations are explicitly forbidden to keep the reader focused on content.
- **Hosting:** Standard static hosting on Netlify.
